Seat Belt Latches

I have it on good authority that 40 years of heat cycles, moisture, sunlight, and plain old use can really destroy a plastic seatbelt. Feel free to ignore the check engine light, or that rattling noise, but the seatbelt can save your life.

It's not that hard to do either, all it takes is a simple butter knife, and extraordinary patience. As they say, it's all in the wrist. Hawks can set you up with a kit, www.hawksmotorsports.com
