Jenny Logo

So a friend of mine is a superstar on the Cricut. When I showed her this design she was immediately on board.

While I was waiting for her to plug in my design, and trim out my logo, I made a paper print out, and worked on positioning. I had options including the front fender and hatch, but landed on the rear quarter.

The design is the easy part, as is figuring out the placement. The hard part and nobody prepared me for it was the difficulty of getting the pattern onto the transfer tape.

You have to take a sheet of sticky "paper" and transfer the design onto it, so it can then be stuck onto the desired surface. This was the most tedious task ever and required a razor blade, and 40 minutes per side.

It also wouldn't hurt to clean and de-grease the surface well.
