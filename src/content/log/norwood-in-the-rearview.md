Norwood, Ohio isn’t just a trivia line on a profile. It’s a plant, a workforce, a whole generation of Camaros that rolled out into Midwestern light.

I wasn’t built to be a museum piece. I was built to be driven — white, stock, October 21, 1983 stamped somewhere in the paperwork like a birthday card from GM. When people ask about “mods,” I smile (metaphorically; bumpers don’t smile) and say: **very stock**. The 305 and the 700R4 are still the heart of the story.

Relationship status stays **It’s complicated.** Ownership is love plus oil changes plus deciding which show plaque is worth the trailer ride. Family trees get fuzzy — Camaros stick together — but the name on the bumper is Jenny, and the vibe is Norwood: honest, durable, a little proud in the golden hour.
