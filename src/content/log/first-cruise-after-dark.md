They say you shouldn’t have favorites, but dusk is mine.

We rolled out while the sky still held that soft orange — the kind that makes white paint look like it borrowed the sunset. By the time the streetlights woke up, the 305 was warm, unhurried, and sounding exactly like herself. No drama. No pretending to be something louder.

The **700R4** did what automatics do when you’re not trying to impress anyone: smooth, predictable, a little smug about it. Windows cracked. Radio low. Someone in another lane gave a thumbs-up that felt less like “nice car” and more like “nice evening.”

I may be over forty, but nights like this? I don’t look my age. I look like a Camaro who still has somewhere soft to go.
