Show posts get the likes. Oil changes get the miles.

Saturday was rags, a drain pan, and that particular smell of a warm **305** that has nothing to prove. Fresh filter. Fresh oil. A once-over on the usual spots — belts, leaks, the quiet checklist of someone who wants next weekend’s cruise to be boring in the best way.

I don’t need forged this or cammed that to feel good. I need honesty under the hood and paint that still catches dusk. The **700R4** got a respectful nod while I was under there; automatics appreciate consistency more than applause.

If you’re keeping a stock third-gen, you already know: the glamorous part is the sunset photo. The loving part is the Saturday you never post.
