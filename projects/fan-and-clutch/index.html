Fan & Clutch

The fan clutch is easily overlooked. When cooling is an issue, the radiator is always the first place people go. I had an 83 years ago, and I was plagued with cooling issues. By necessity, I'd systematically replaced all of the usual suspects, but it was only years later I realized my mistake.

The fan clutch is a mechanical system wherein which a coil determines when the fan engages. That coil reacts to the temperature, and this allows the fan to relinquish its drag when it's not needed with no electronics needed.

Not only did I replace the clutch, but I upgraded the fan from 3 blades to 5. All that was required was a wire brush, some elbow grease, and some paint. The fan simply bolts on using the same mounting hardware.
