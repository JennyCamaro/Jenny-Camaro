Underglow

I bought these clips hoping the light strip would fit in them. Using a clip gets the strip off of the body enough to use a wire tie. Most of these lights come with double sided tape, but I don't trust that. Wire ties provide two benefits, strength, and the ability to easily remove the lights for any maintenance.

I drilled and tapped new holes, and used machine screws, and lock washers for a clean and complete job. Each light strip is held in place by several of these clips, and wire ties.
